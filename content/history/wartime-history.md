# The Wartime History of the Offices of the Supervisor of Shipbuilding, USN

**Capt. E. L. Patch**, USN, Supervisor of Shipbuilding, USN, Bay City, Michigan. 27 May 1946.

*BuShips letters EN/A3-2(130), EM28/A2-11 of 11 May 1945, and EN/A3-2(130) of 11 January 1946.*

## About this document

The report covers the Offices of the Supervisor of Shipbuilding, USN, at Bay City and Detroit — not Defoe alone. Patch prepared it in Bay City on 27 May 1946, pursuant to those Bureau of Ships letters. In Part I, Defoe Shipbuilding Company is one contractor among Fisher, Chris-Craft, Eddy, and the other lakes yards under those offices.
